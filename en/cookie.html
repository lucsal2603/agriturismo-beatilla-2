Cookie policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

This Cookie Policy describes the types of cookies and tracking tools used by the website www.agriturismobeatilla.it (the “Site”), owned by Beatilla Società Agricola S.S., and explains how to give, manage or withdraw consent to their use.

1. What cookies and tracking tools are

Cookies are small text files that the sites you visit send to your device (browser), where they are stored and then sent back to the same sites on your next visit. In addition to cookies, the Site may use other tracking tools or analytical trackers (e.g. tracking pixels, tags or scripts) to collect information about how you interact with the page.

2. Types of cookies used by the Site

A. Technical and necessary cookies (no consent required)

These are the cookies that are essential for browsing and for the basic features of the Site to work properly. They make it possible, for example, to save your browsing preferences or session. Under art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR), installing these cookies does not require your prior consent.

B. Analytics and statistics cookies (Google Analytics 4 — consent required)

These cookies measure visitor numbers and analyse traffic on the Site in aggregate, statistical form. The Site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand how users interact with the website's content and improve its performance and usability.

  • Provider: Google Ireland Limited
  • Purpose: statistics, site performance measurement and traffic analysis.
  • Legal basis: the user's express consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR).

C. Advertising and marketing cookies (Google Ads — consent required)

These cookies and tracking pixels are used to track conversions and the effectiveness of advertising campaigns run on Google Ads, as well as to show personalised ads or carry out remarketing towards users who have previously visited the Site.

  • Provider: Google Ireland Limited
  • Purpose: advertising conversion tracking, remarketing and commercial profiling.
  • Legal basis: the user's express consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR).

D. Tag and script management (Google Tag Manager)

The Site uses Google Tag Manager for the centralised management and activation of measurement and marketing scripts. The service controls the release of tags and cookies in strict accordance with the choices made by the user (supporting Google Consent Mode v2).

E. Technology script for AI visibility (dAirect)

The Site integrates the dAirect service script (provided by Takyon S.r.l.), used solely for the technical structuring of the farm's public data to optimise its indexing and visibility on search engines and Artificial Intelligence assistants (GEO/AI search). This script does not carry out any commercial profiling of the user.

3. Summary of tracking tools

  • Technical Cookies (Beatilla S.S.): Saving of language and reading preferences (Session / Persistent).
  • Cookie Consent (Banner Platform): Saving of privacy consent choices (Duration: 12 months).
  • Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*): Statistical traffic analysis (Duration: up to 2 years, subject to consent).
  • Google Ads Conversion & Remarketing: Campaign and ad measurement (Duration: up to 90 days, subject to consent).
  • dAirect Script (Takyon S.r.l.): Technical optimisation for AI engines (No profiling).

4. How to manage or withdraw cookie consent

On your first visit to the Site, a cookie banner is shown that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies: giving consent to the installation of all tools (including GA4 and Google Ads);
  • Reject non-essential cookies: keeping only the essential technical cookies active;
  • Customise your choices: selecting in detail which categories of cookies to allow.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking the “Cookie Preferences” link in the footer of the Site, or by setting your preferences directly in your web browser (deleting stored cookies).

About us

On the edge of Mantua, beside the historic Bosco della Fontana and within the Mincio Park, Beatilla is a place where art, nature and eco-design meet.

More than a farm stay, it is a place made for anyone who wants to slow down, breathe and live the countryside for what it really is.

Every room comes from the meeting of natural materials, creative reuse, colour and handmade detail. The project was conceived by the international artist and eco-designer Luca Bornoffi, who brought here the hospitality concept he developed working with luxury hotels around the world, rediscovering the «homemade» style — one of the most sustainable farm stays in the area.

Beatilla offers apartments and rooms surrounded by green, made to welcome couples, families and travellers looking for quiet. Minutes from Mantua and on the Mantua–Peschiera cycle path, our farmhouse is the ideal base to explore the area — or simply to stop for a while.

Our motto

«Mi casa es tu casa»

A meeting of colours where art, nature, eco-design, reuse and hospitality are the heart of our story. Our mission is to make you feel at home — and to give you a smile.

Art Residency

More than an artist residency. A place to create, share and belong.

Beatilla Art Residency was created to give artists and creative professionals the time, the space and the quiet they need to develop their own research. Set in the countryside near Mantua, it is a place where art, nature and eco-design live side by side every day, turning ordinary life into a source of inspiration.

We welcome artists from every discipline, supporting both individual work and cultural exchange through shared experiences, hospitality and rural life.

The artist

«We often forget where colours come from.»

Luca Bornoffi (Mantua, 1980) is a contemporary artist whose work grows out of a deep bond with nature. Raised in the countryside, among horses and rural landscapes, he developed a particular feeling for colour, understood as a source of harmony, energy and wellbeing.

His practice takes shape in the Circles of Life series: abstract compositions of circles, transparencies and colours that evoke connection, encounter and the imperfect rhythm of life. For Bornoffi colour is not only something you see, but a language able to change how spaces and emotions are perceived.

After travelling in more than 60 countries, he created Beatilla Holiday Farm Eco Design, a place decorated entirely with his own work and furnished with reclaimed pieces. His works are part of collections and exhibitions worldwide and are listed on Artsy, Artnet and Artsper.

The area

What to see

Mantua, a UNESCO World Heritage city and Italian Capital of Culture in 2016, is one of the most beautiful cities in Italy: palaces, museums, churches and squares tell centuries of Gonzaga art and history. Do not miss the famous pumpkin tortelli and the traditional sbrisolona cake, true highlights of Mantuan cooking.

5 km · 5 min

Mantua

The Ducal Palace with Mantegna’s Camera degli Sposi, Palazzo Te, Piazza delle Erbe, the Rotonda di San Lorenzo and the Basilica of Sant’Andrea: an open-air museum.

On foot

Bosco della Fontana

The largest forest of the Po Valley, with the sixteenth-century hunting lodge of the Gonzaga family. You can walk there straight from the farm.

~20 min

Borghetto sul Mincio

Reachable by bike from Beatilla. One of the most beautiful villages in Italy, with watermills, old lanes and views over the river Mincio.

~30 min

Verona

The city of Romeo and Juliet, a UNESCO site, with the Arena, elegant squares and a historic centre full of charm.

~25 min

Peschiera del Garda

Reachable by bike from Beatilla. A fortified UNESCO town where canals, Venetian bastions and Lake Garda meet.

~30 min

Sirmione

The pearl of Lake Garda, with the Scaliger Castle, the Grotte di Catullo and its renowned thermal baths.

Also minutes away: the Morainic Hills with their vineyards, Parco Giardino Sigurtà, one of the finest gardens in Europe, and Gardaland for younger guests.

Getting here

Where we are

From the A22 motorway (Modena–Brenner)

Exit at Mantova Nord and follow signs for Brescia. After about 8 km, at the roundabout continue towards Brescia; after 500 m turn left towards Bosco Fontana and take the first right after 200 m.

From Mantua

Head towards Brescia. At Cittadella turn left for Brescia – Lake Garda; after about 3 km, at the roundabout continue straight towards Brescia. After 500 m turn left towards Bosco Fontana, then take the first right after 200 m.

From Brescia

Follow the SS Goitese towards Mantua: after Montichiari, Castiglione delle Stiviere and Goito, pass Marmirolo. After about 3 km turn right towards Bosco Fontana and take the first right after 200 m.

Tip: set your satnav to “Corte Barco”.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is available from 15:00 and check-out is by 10:00. So that we can prepare your stay with the greatest care, we kindly ask you to respect the arrival time.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Every morning we bring you our Breakfast Basket, prepared with fresh homemade produce and delivered straight to your room at the time you choose, between 7:30 and 9:45.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free open-air parking is available on site. Covered parking is also available on request, for a fee.

Is the restaurant open to people not staying at Beatilla?

Yes. The restaurant is happy to welcome outside guests too, by reservation. To book: +39 339 222 6468.

Is Beatilla suitable for families with children?

Absolutely. We have cots for younger children, wide outdoor spaces and a large garden for relaxed family time.

Are pets allowed?

No, at the moment we are not able to host pets.

Can I hire a bike?

Yes. Bikes are available on request, ideal for exploring the Bosco della Fontana, Mantua, Borghetto sul Mincio and the cycle path to Lake Garda.

Where is Beatilla?

Beatilla is in Marmirolo, in the heart of the countryside near Mantua, minutes from the historic centre and from the Bosco della Fontana. It is an ideal base for the area between Mantua, Lake Garda and the morainic hills.

Do the apartments have a kitchen?

Yes. All apartments have a fully equipped kitchen with crockery and appliances.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes, Wi-Fi is free and available throughout the property.

Can I book experiences during my stay?

Yes. We arrange art workshops, farm visits, excursions, bike hire and other experiences on request.

Still have a question? Get in touch — we will be glad to help.
